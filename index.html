Principles

The boring rules that make this legitimate.

There's already a gray market for "cheap Claude tokens" — pooled accounts, stolen credentials, silently swapped models, prompts harvested and resold. Overflow exists to be the opposite of that, structurally:

✕No account sharing, ever. We never touch your Claude, OpenAI, or any provider credentials — not yours, not sellers'. Nothing on the platform violates a provider's terms.
✕No silent substitution. Every response is stamped with the exact model that produced it. Workers are canary-tested so a seller can't serve a smaller model than advertised.
✕No prompt harvesting. Requests are metered, not logged. Your prompts are not a product.
✓Hard caps and instant revocation. Sellers set a token ceiling and a time window; the meter is authoritative and the revoke button works mid-holiday, from a phone, in seconds.
✓Open-weight models, openly licensed. The pool serves models whose weights are licensed for exactly this. Proprietary models join only via official partner channels.
